Privacy policy

Trancecribe for iPhone and iPad · Effective October 11, 2026

Short version: your lectures never come to us. There's no account. We count which features get used, and if you came from a TikTok ad, TikTok learns about the install and any purchase. You can turn both off in the app.

No account

Trancecribe has no sign-up and no login. We don't know who you are.

Your lectures stay yours

Recordings, transcripts, summaries, photos and flashcards are stored on your device. Transcription and summaries are made on the device itself using Apple's built-in speech recognition and Apple Intelligence. Your lectures are never sent to us or to any AI service.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is on for Trancecribe, your courses and notes sync through your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. With audio sync on, recordings sync too. This data sits in your iCloud storage under Apple's privacy policy. We can't access it. You can turn sync off in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud, and delete the synced data in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The app only learns whether a pass is active. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Permissions

The microphone records lectures, the camera snaps the board or slides, and photo access attaches slides you choose. Each is used only for that, and only when you ask.

Analytics and tracking

To see what helps students and what doesn't, the app sends usage events: for example that a recording started, that notes were ready, that a flashcard was reviewed or that the paywall was opened, with counts like the lecture's length in minutes. Events never contain lecture audio, transcripts, summaries, titles or course names.

  • PostHog (product analytics, US servers) receives these events with a random ID. If you're signed in to iCloud, the ID is a one-way hash of your iCloud user record for this app, so your iPhone and iPad count as one person; it doesn't reveal your name, email or Apple Account.
  • TikTok (ad measurement) receives the install, app opens, onboarding finished, first notes ready and purchases, so we can tell which TikTok ads bring students in. TikTok gets your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, it receives no advertising identifier.

Turn all of this off in the app under Settings › Share usage data. We don't sell data, and we don't show ads.

Recording other people

Lectures include your professor and classmates. Follow your school's recording policy and keep recordings for your own studying.

Children

Trancecribe is meant for students and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect anyone's personal information.

Deleting your data

Delete a lecture or course inside the app, or delete the app. Synced copies can be removed from iCloud as described above. Usage events are tied only to the random ID described above. To have them deleted, contact us with the date you installed the app and we'll remove what we can match.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the change ships.
