Support

Trancecribe for iPhone and iPad

Questions, bugs or ideas: email mmykolapopovv@gmail.com. Include your device model and the app version (Settings › About).

Which devices does it work on?

Recording works on any iPhone or iPad running iOS 26. Transcripts need iPhone 12 or newer. Summaries and exam prep use Apple Intelligence: iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or an iPad with M1 or A17 Pro or newer, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Recording, transcripts and summaries all happen on your device.

My summary hasn't appeared

Processing takes roughly a tenth of the lecture's length. It keeps going in the background, and you'll get a notification when it's ready. If the app was force-quit, it picks up where it left off the next time you open it.

Recording stopped early

Force-quitting the app (swiping it away) stops a recording, and iOS doesn't let apps prevent that. Locking the phone or switching apps is fine.

My lectures aren't on my iPad

Make sure both devices use the same Apple ID and iCloud is on for Trancecribe (iOS Settings › your name › iCloud). Text syncs for everyone. Audio sync is part of Pro.

Restore a purchase

Settings › Restore purchases (or Restore on the paywall). Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Cancel the Annual plan

iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Trancecribe. The Semester Pass never renews, so there's nothing to cancel.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
